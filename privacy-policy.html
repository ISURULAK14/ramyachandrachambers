Privacy Policy & Client Confidentiality

Professional Legal Privilege • Statutory Confidentiality • Zero Commercial Profiling

Ramyachandra Gunasekera Chambers is dedicated to upholding the strictest standards of legal confidentiality, ethical advocacy, and professional privilege under the statutes of Sri Lanka, the Evidence Ordinance (Sections 126–129), and the Bar Association guidelines.

Information Handling & Attorney-Client Privilege

All correspondence, consultation requests, title deeds, Land Registry extracts, court briefs, and confidential case particulars submitted via digital channels, telephone, or during in-person chamber sessions at our Fort Head Office and Kotavila Branch Office are protected under professional Attorney-Client privilege. Instructions and legal files are handled directly by Senior Attorney Ramyachandra Gunasekera and authorized chamber staff.

Strict Privacy & Data Minimization Guarantee

This website operates under an absolute privacy model: zero commercial advertising networks, zero tracking cookies, zero data broking, and zero third-party behavioral profiling trackers. We enforce referrer suppression and client origin privacy to prevent cross-site surveillance.

Transport Security & Communications Integrity

Digital connectivity to this site is fortified with modern 256-bit TLS encryption, strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS Preload with 2-year duration), and hardened Content Security Policies (CSP) to eliminate cross-site scripting and unauthorized content injection.

Contact for Privacy & Verification Inquiries

For questions regarding our privacy practices, client data verification, or security policies, contact Chambers directly at info@ramyachandrachambers.com or visit our offices in Matara, Sri Lanka.
