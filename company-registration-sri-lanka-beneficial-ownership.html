Private Limited Company Registration & Beneficial Ownership Compliance

Statutory procedure under Companies Act No. 07 of 2007, DRC e-ROC digital incorporation, Form 1/18/19 filings, and the new mandatory Beneficial Ownership regulations.

1. Corporate Legal Framework in Sri Lanka

In Sri Lanka, the incorporation, administration, and governance of corporate bodies is governed by the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 (as amended) and regulated by the Department of the Registrar of Companies (DRC). A Private Limited Company (PVT LTD) is the preeminent vehicle for domestic enterprises, foreign direct investment (FDI), and tourism ventures across the Southern Province (Matara, Mirissa, Weligama, Galle).

A Private Limited Company offers limited liability protection, perpetual legal succession, and the ability to hold real estate, enter binding contracts, and issue shares. Under Sri Lankan law, a private company must have at least one shareholder and one director (with at least one resident director), and is statutorily required to appoint a qualified Registered Company Secretary.

2. Step-by-Step DRC e-ROC Incorporation Procedure

Step 1: Name Search & Reservation

The proposed corporate name must be checked against the DRC database via the official e-ROC portal. The name cannot be misleadingly similar to an existing entity and must end with the suffix “(Private) Limited” or “(Pvt) Ltd”. Once approved, the DRC reserves the name for three (3) months.

Step 2: Articles of Association

Under Section 13 of the Act, every company must have Articles of Association establishing internal management rules, share transfer restrictions (pre-emptive rights), and board governance. Companies may adopt the standard statutory Model Articles (Table A of the First Schedule) or execute customized Articles prepared by an Attorney-at-Law to safeguard investor equity or joint-venture covenants.

Step 3: Statutory Incorporation Forms (Form 1, 18, 19)

Three core statutory forms must be prepared, signed by the respective parties, and submitted through the e-ROC system:

  • Form 1 (Application for Registration): Discloses the registered office address, primary business activities, initial stated capital, and number of shares.
  • Form 18 (Consent and Certificate of Director): Signed by each appointed director declaring consent to act and certifying that they are not disqualified under Section 202.
  • Form 19 (Consent and Certificate of Secretary): Signed by a licensed Registered Company Secretary or legal firm consenting to act and citing their official DRC registration warrant.

3. New Mandatory Beneficial Ownership (BO) Transparency Rules

In accordance with the Companies (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 2023 and national commitments to international financial transparency (Financial Action Task Force • FATF standards), Sri Lanka enacted mandatory Beneficial Ownership (BO) disclosure requirements for all companies incorporated in Sri Lanka.

Who is a Beneficial Owner?

Under the regulations, a Beneficial Owner is defined as any natural person (individual) who ultimately owns or controls a company. Specifically, an individual who:

  • Holds directly or indirectly ten percent (10%) or more of the shares or voting rights in the company.
  • Exercises ultimate effective control over the company through debt instruments, family arrangements, voting trusts, or nominee agreements.
  • Holds the authority to appoint or remove a majority of the Board of Directors.

Mandatory BO Filing Obligations:

  • Register of Beneficial Owners: Every company must establish and maintain an internal Register of Beneficial Owners at its registered office address.
  • Submission to DRC: Accurate details (full legal name, passport/NIC number, residential address, nationality, tax residency, and the exact nature/percentage of beneficial interest) must be filed with the Registrar General of Companies during incorporation and updated within 14 days of any change.
  • Non-Compliance Penalties: Failure to identify and declare beneficial owners carries severe statutory financial penalties on the company, its directors, and the company secretary, and can result in administrative suspension on the e-ROC portal.

4. Post-Incorporation Compliance Checklist

  1. Certificate of Incorporation (PV Number): Issued digitally by the Registrar of Companies alongside the certified Articles of Association.
  2. Inland Revenue Department (IRD) TIN Registration: Compulsory registration for a corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) at the Inland Revenue Department.
  3. Corporate Bank Account Opening: Requiring certified true copies of Form 1, Form 18, Form 19, Certificate of Incorporation, Articles, and formal Board Resolutions.
  4. Statutory Registers: Maintenance of Share Register, Register of Directors & Secretaries, Register of Charges, and Register of Beneficial Owners.
  5. Annual Returns (Form 20): Mandatory annual filing with the DRC within 30 days of the company’s Annual General Meeting.

Official Regulatory & Corporate Portals

  • Department of the Registrar of Companies (DRC e-ROC Portal)
  • Inland Revenue Department Sri Lanka • Corporate TIN & Tax Guidelines
  • Board of Investment of Sri Lanka (BOI) • Foreign Direct Investment
