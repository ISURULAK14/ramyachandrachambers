Profile & Credentials

Discover the history and expertise behind Ramyachandra Gunasekera Chambers.

Over 37 Years of Practice

A Legacy of Legal Precision

Establishing a standard of excellence over 37+ years of practice, Ramyachandra Gunasekera has built a reputation founded on rigorous attention to detail, uncompromising professional integrity, and an unparalleled understanding of the Sri Lankan legal landscape.

The firm has successfully facilitated the structuring and operation of over 300+ registered companies, acting as a vital conduit for both local enterprise and international foreign investment traversing through the Southern Province.

Operating out of fully staffed offices in Matara Fort and Kamburugamuwa, with a residential base in Colombo 7, the practice continues to cater to clients across 40+ countries who require dependable counsel in complex legal frameworks.

RAMYACHANDRA GUNASEKERA

Professional Roles & Admissions

  • Attorney-at-Law: Standing advocate offering comprehensive legal representation across civil litigation and fundamental rights applications within the Sri Lankan judicial system.
  • Notary Public: Licensed official executing critical real estate conveyancing, deeds of transfer, and powers of attorney for domestic and non-resident clients.
  • Registered Company Secretary: Authorized practitioner managing corporate compliance, ROC filings, and structuring under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007.

Scale & Reach

  • 37+ Years of Practice: A proven track record in Matara, Galle, Tangalle, and Hambantota courts.
  • 300+ Registered Companies: Extensive experience handling incorporation, compliance, and shareholder structuring.
  • International Client Base: Serving individuals and corporations spanning over 40+ countries requiring trusted on-the-ground legal oversight in Sri Lanka.
