Legal Services & Expertise

Six core areas, refined over thirty-eight years of instruction from landowners, families, employers and companies across the Southern Province.

Property & Conveyancing

Title investigation, deeds of transfer and gift, partition, prescription, leases and the full notarial execution of land transactions.

  • Deeds of transfer, gift & declaration
  • Partition & co-ownership disputes
Company & Commercial
▼

Incorporation, company secretarial services, shareholder and joint-venture arrangements, and the commercial agreements that keep a business defensible.

  • Company incorporation & ROC filings
  • Registered company secretary
  • BO Registration (Beneficial Ownership)

  • Shareholder & director agreements
Notarial Services
▼

Deeds, powers of attorney, affidavits, last wills and attestations, drafted, executed and registered by a Notary Public of long standing.

  • Survey on due diligence
Civil Litigation

Representation before the District and Magistrate's Courts of Matara, Galle, Tangalle and Hambantota in land, contract, debt and family matters.

  • Contract & debt recovery
  • Testamentary & family applications
Labour & Employment

Contracts of service, disciplinary procedure, terminations, EPF/ETF questions and appearances before the Labour Tribunal.

  • Employment contracts & handbooks
  • Termination & disciplinary advice
  • Labour Tribunal representation
Foreign Investment

Advice for overseas clients acquiring or holding Sri Lankan assets, together with guidance for non-resident powers of attorney and foreign investment consultancy.

  • Foreign investment structuring
  • Non-resident powers of attorney
  • Foreign investment consultancy
