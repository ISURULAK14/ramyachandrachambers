Practice Disciplines

Comprehensive Legal & Corporate Services

Delivering rigorous advocacy, dependable notarial conveyancing, and statutory corporate secretarial services across the Southern Province and Sri Lanka.

Court Litigation & Advocacy

Expert courtroom representation in civil disputes, commercial litigation, money recovery, injunctions, fundamental rights, and criminal defense.

  • District Court civil litigation & debt recovery
  • High Court commercial actions & bail applications
  • Magistrate Court trial defense & statutory inquiries
  • Partition lawsuits & declaration of title actions

Property Deeds & Conveyancing

Meticulous title investigation and notarial execution of all land transactions in strict compliance with the Notaries Ordinance.

  • Title searches & legal title opinions
  • Deeds of transfer, gift, exchange & declaration
  • Long-term commercial & residential leases
  • Mortgage bonds & loan security agreements

Company Registration & Secretarial

Complete corporate formation and statutory secretarial governance under Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 and eROC portal.

  • Private limited company incorporation (Form 1, 18, 19)
  • Registered Company Secretary annual maintenance
  • Beneficial Ownership (BO) compliance & filings
  • Company Tax Identification Number (TIN) registration

Notarial Attestations & Affidavits

Official authentication of statutory documents, affidavits, commercial agreements, and international consular documents.

  • Statutory declarations & sworn affidavits
  • General & Special Powers of Attorney
  • Consular legalization document certification
  • Commercial contracts & non-disclosure agreements

Family Law, Probate & Estates

Compassionate and confidential counsel for estate distribution, testamentary probate, and family legal matters.

  • Drafting of Last Wills and codicils
  • Testamentary probate & Letters of Administration
  • Matrimonial dispute resolution & custody settlements
  • Estate distribution & inheritance administration

Foreign Investment & Overseas Clients

Dedicated legal counsel for foreign nationals, expatriates, and Sri Lankan diaspora across 35+ countries investing in Sri Lanka.

  • Coastal villa & tourism real estate acquisition
  • Land restrictions for non-citizens compliance
  • Overseas Power of Attorney attestation guidance
  • Cross-border inheritance & repatriation counsel
