Core Disciplines

Specialized Legal Practice Areas

Focused legal representation, rigorous document examination, and statutory compliance across three primary practice pillars.

1. Attorney-at-Law & Court Litigation

Civil and criminal litigation across Southern Province Courts (Matara, Galle, Tangalle) and the Appellate Courts in Colombo. Over 35 years of trial advocacy in land disputes, co-ownership partitions, money claims, and commercial injunctions.

  • Civil Litigation: Land partition lawsuits, declaration of title, boundary disputes, and easement rights.
  • Commercial Law: Breach of contract, recovery of debts, and corporate shareholder disputes.
  • Criminal Defense: Bail applications, trial advocacy in Magistrate Courts and High Courts.

2. Notary Public & Conveyancing

Licensed notarial practice safeguarding real estate transactions through exhaustive title investigations, pedigree tracing, and valid registration at the Land Registry.

  • Title Examination: Examination of prior deeds and register extract volumes to eliminate defect risks.
  • Deed Execution: Deeds of Transfer, Gift, Exchange, Mortgage Bonds, and Condominium declarations.
  • Powers of Attorney: General and Special Powers of Attorney executed for domestic and overseas clients.

3. Registered Company Secretary

Official corporate secretarial services under the Department of Registrar of Companies (DRC) ensuring full compliance with the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007.

  • Company Formation: Complete incorporation process from name approval to Form 1 registration.
  • Statutory Filings: Annual Returns (Form 15), Directorship changes (Form 20), Share Transfers (Form 6).
  • Tax & BO Compliance: Company TIN registration, DRC Beneficial Ownership compliance records.
