Client Privacy & Data Protection

Ramyachandra Gunasekera Chambers is bound by the strict statutory duties of legal professional privilege and advocate-client confidentiality under Sri Lankan law.

Any documentation, personal data, title deeds, or instructions submitted to our chambers are maintained under strict security and used exclusively for legal representation, deed attestation, or company filings.
