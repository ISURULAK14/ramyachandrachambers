Company Registration & Secretary

Corporate Services in Matara

Professional Company Secretarial services ensuring strict compliance with the Registrar of Companies (eROC) and the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007.

Problem: Need to incorporate a business, file annual returns, or update corporate directorships.
Requirement: A Registered Company Secretary and adherence to eROC filings.
Solution: We act as your Registered Company Secretary. We handle name reservations, draft Articles of Association, and file Forms 01, 18, and 19. We also manage Beneficial Ownership (BO) compliance and shareholder resolutions.
Verification: The company maintains full regulatory compliance and active legal status in Sri Lanka.

Streamline your corporate compliance. Consult our Registered Company Secretary.
