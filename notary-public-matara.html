Notary Public in Matara

Deeds, Affidavits & Powers of Attorney

With over 35 years of distinguished standing, Ramyachandra Gunasekera serves as a trusted Notary Public in Matara and the Southern Province.

Problem: Need legally binding property transfers, certified documents, or delegated authority.
Requirement: A registered Notary Public in the relevant jurisdiction.
Solution: We draft and attest all kinds of deeds and legal agreements in Sri Lanka, including Deeds of Transfer, Gift, Declaration, and Partition alongside Lease Agreements, Mortgage Bonds, Commercial Contracts, Shareholder Agreements, and Powers of Attorney. We also prepare General and Special Powers of Attorney for overseas clients.
Verification: Documents are properly stamped, registered at the Land Registry, and legally certified for domestic and international use.

Appointments are available at both our Matara Fort Head Office and Kamburugamuwa Branch Office. Contact us to schedule a consultation.
