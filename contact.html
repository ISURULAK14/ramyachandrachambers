Privacy Policy & Client Confidentiality

Strict Legal Professional Privilege: All communications, case documents, title deeds, corporate records, and personal identification submitted to Ramyachandra Gunasekera Chambers are protected by statutory Attorney-Client Privilege under the Evidence Ordinance of Sri Lanka.

Data Protection: In accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, we maintain physical, procedural, and technological safeguards to protect client records.

Non-Disclosure: We never sell, lease, or distribute client contact details or consultation records to any third party or marketing entity.

Terms of Engagement

Professional Scope: Ramyachandra Gunasekera Chambers provides legal advocacy, notary public attestations, title searches, and registered company secretarial services.

Retainer & Consultation: Initial website reviews or appointment inquiries do not constitute a formal advocate-client retainer until formal acceptance and instructions are executed.

Notarial Compliance: All deeds of transfer, gift, mortgage, and affidavits are executed in strict accordance with the Notaries Ordinance and Land Registration laws of Sri Lanka.
